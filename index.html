💡 Flight booking tips from WanderWise
Best booking time: 6–8 weeks for international, 3–4 weeks for domestic. Tuesday/Wednesday flights are usually 15–20% cheaper than weekends.

Open-jaw trick: Fly into one city, out of another — often cheaper and avoids backtracking (e.g. Mumbai→Lisbon + Barcelona→Mumbai).
Use incognito mode when searching — flight sites track your searches and raise prices.

Best airlines from India: Turkish Airlines and Qatar Airways consistently offer the best India-Europe fares. Air India is best for direct long-haul. IndiGo for Southeast Asia.
